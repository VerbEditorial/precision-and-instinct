23 August 2010 · Pimlico, central London

Spy in a Bag: The Death of Gareth Williams

Open Case A 2021–2023 Metropolitan Police forensic review found no new DNA and no new leads. Police say he most likely died alone, but that the case “cannot be resolved beyond reasonable doubt.” His family rejects the accident finding.

Gareth Williams, a 31-year-old GCHQ mathematician seconded to MI6, was found inside a padlocked holdall in the bath of his flat. This is what Eli and Simone argued over.

Nobody raised the alarm

The missing week

Eight days passed between the last sighting and the day police opened his door.

  • Last confirmed sighting, on CCTV15 Aug, about 3pm
  • Expected at workNo alarm raised
  • Police check the flat23 Aug
  1. 01 / 04 · THE BAG

    A red holdall, padlocked, in the bath of his flat.

  2. 02 / 04 · THE FLAT

    No forced entry. No fingerprints. No sign the flat had been cleaned.

  3. 03 / 04 · THE DNA

    Traces from two unknown people on the bag, too degraded to build a profile.

  4. 04 / 04 · THE TEST

    At the 2012 inquest, escape artists made about 400 attempts to lock themselves into the same type of bag. None managed it.

Two official answers

Same evidence
May 2012 · The inquest

“Likely” unlawfully killed.

Coroner Dr Fiona Wilcox recorded a narrative verdict of death by unnatural causes, and criticised MI6 for delaying its cooperation with police.

November 2013 · Metropolitan Police

“Probably an accident.”

No other person likely present, while acknowledging “evidential contradictions and gaps in our understanding.”

Nobody has ever been charged.

Two verdicts, still no answer

2010 to now
  1. 201015 AUGCCTV records the last confirmed sighting of him, at about 3pm.
  2. 201023 AUGPolice find him in a padlocked holdall in the bath of his Pimlico flat.
  3. 2012MAYThe inquest finds it likely he was unlawfully killed, and criticises MI6.

Two readings

No winner
Precision · Eli Marchetti

“Four hundred attempts by trained professionals is data.”

  • “Theoretically possible is not the same as demonstrated.”
  • “That’s an intelligence agency slow-walking a murder investigation into one of its own people.”
  • “Every explanation on the table is, on its own terms, unlikely.”
Instinct · Simone Valdez

“Undetectable assassinations are rare precisely because they’re hard to pull off cleanly.”

  • “The people whose entire job is noticing things — didn’t notice him.”
  • “An escape-artist trial under studio conditions, with cameras and observers and no adrenaline, is not automatically the same as knowing what one specific, motivated person could do alone…”
  • “Or it’s an institution correcting an inquest verdict that leaned harder on ‘impossible’ than the evidence actually supported.”

In their own words, from Episode 12.

No verdict.

  3. How does an intelligence agency not notice one of its own is missing for over a week?
