1 December 1948 · Somerton Park beach, Adelaide

The Somerton Man

Open Case A 2022 DNA study named the man as Carl "Charles" Webb. South Australia Police finished their own DNA analysis in December 2023, but the Coroner has not issued a formal finding. How he died is still unknown.

John Lyons and his companions found him reclined against the seawall as if asleep, a half-smoked cigarette resting on his collar. This is what Eli and Simone argued over.

Tamám Shud

Persian for "ended." Sewn into a hidden pocket in his trousers, torn from the last page of a 1941 Rubáiyát of Omar Khayyám. The book itself turned up in an unlocked car near the beach.

Also on the cover: an unlisted phone number, traced to nurse Jessica "Jo" Thomson, who lived about 400 metres from where he was found.

Never deciphered. A 2014 study found it consistent with the first letters of English words.
